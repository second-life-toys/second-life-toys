## What this project is

This is a hobby project made for people who already own the Sphero Spider-Man interactive toy. The manufacturer discontinued the official app and shut down the servers the toy relied on, which left many of these toys unable to finish setup or play. Second Life Toys exists so that hardware people already bought does not become e-waste. It provides free companion apps and a replacement service that stands in for the servers that are gone.
